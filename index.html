Quick Reduction Tips

Evidence-based strategies to lower your carbon footprint starting today.

🚶

Walk Short Distances

Replacing car trips under 2km with walking saves up to 0.4 kg CO₂ per trip.

Saves ~150 kg CO₂/year
🥦

One Meatless Day

Skipping meat one day per week reduces food emissions by approximately 14%.

Saves ~200 kg CO₂/year
🌡️

Adjust Thermostat

Lowering heating by 1°C saves about 300 kg CO₂ annually on average.

Saves ~300 kg CO₂/year
✈️

Fly Less

One fewer round-trip transatlantic flight saves roughly 1.6 tonnes of CO₂.

Saves ~1,600 kg CO₂/year
💧

Cold Water Wash

Washing clothes at 30°C instead of 60°C uses 40% less energy per load.

Saves ~100 kg CO₂/year
💡

Switch to LEDs

LED bulbs use 75% less energy than incandescent and last 25 times longer.

Saves ~50 kg CO₂/year
